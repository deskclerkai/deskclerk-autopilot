# Trademark and Brand Policy

This policy covers the **DeskClerk**, **DeskClerk AI** and **DeskClerk AI Autopilot** names, the DeskClerk logo, and the visual identity of this project.

It is separate from the code license. The code is licensed under Apache-2.0; the trademarks are not.

## Why this file exists

Apache-2.0 gives everyone the right to use, modify and redistribute the code. It does **not** give anyone the right to use the project's names or logo as the branding of their own product.

A common misconception is that an open-source license can require every fork to display the original logo and a link back. It cannot: adding that condition would make the license no longer a standard open-source license, and most companies would avoid the project. We therefore keep the code license clean and put brand rules here.

## What you may do without asking

- State truthfully that your project is "based on DeskClerk AI Autopilot" or a "fork of DeskClerk AI Autopilot".
- Link to this repository.
- Keep the original name, logo and copyright notices when you redistribute an **unmodified** copy.
- Use the name in articles, tutorials, comparisons and academic work that refer to the project.
- Use the logo in a screenshot of the unmodified product.

## What you may not do

- Use the DeskClerk name or logo as the name or logo of a modified product or a competing service.
- Imply that your fork, deployment or service is official, endorsed or sponsored by DeskClerk.
- Register a domain, company name, app name or social account that is confusingly similar to DeskClerk.
- Remove or alter copyright, license or trademark notices from an unmodified distribution.

## Attribution request

If you fork the project or publish a deployment, we ask you to keep the DeskClerk AI name, a link to this repository and, where practical, the logo. This is a **request**, not an additional condition of the Apache-2.0 license.

## Commercial branding

A separate brand license is available for hosted services, managed deployments and commercial distributions that want to use the official DeskClerk name or logo. Contact the maintainers through the repository.
